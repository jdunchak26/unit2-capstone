EMPLOYEE COMPLAINT RECORD
Company: Northstar Services
Complaint Reference: EMP-2026-001
Date Submitted: October 8, 2026
Submitted By: Employee 4
Department: Technology
Received By: Human Resources
Status: Under Review

Complaint Summary

Employee 4 submitted a workplace complaint regarding repeated disrespectful communication from a supervisor during team meetings and one-on-one discussions.
Employee 4 stated that the interrupted them, dismissed their questions, and used an inappropriate tone when discussing work assignments. Employee 4
also reported feeling uncomfortable raising concerns directly with the supervisor because previous attempts to do so did not resolve the issue.

Details Provided by the Employee

The employee reported that:

The behavior occurred during several team meetings.
The supervisor made dismissive comments about the employee’s questions.
The employee was interrupted while explaining a work-related issue.
Similar conduct occurred during a private discussion about workload.
The situation affected the employee’s confidence and ability to participate in meetings.
The employee is requesting a fair review and a respectful working environment.


Documents and Information Provided

The employee provided:

A written summary of the incidents
Dates of two team meetings
Copies of relevant email communications
Names of two potential witnesses


Initial Actions Taken

Human Resources acknowledged receipt of the complaint on October 8, 2026.
The following initial actions were taken:

The complaint was assigned a reference number.
The employee was informed that the matter would be reviewed.
The employee was reminded of the company’s non-retaliation policy.
Relevant communications were preserved.
The matter was assigned to an HR representative for review.


Planned Review Activities

The HR representative plans to:

Meet with the employee to clarify the reported incidents.
Review the documents provided.
Speak with identified witnesses.
Meet with the supervisor to obtain a response.
Review relevant company policies.
Determine whether additional interim measures are appropriate.
Document the outcome and recommended next steps.


Confidentiality

The complaint will be handled as confidentially as practical. Information may be shared with individuals who need it to conduct the review, protect employees, or determine appropriate action.
Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed because the review may require discussions with witnesses and the supervisor.

Non-Retaliation Notice

The company prohibits retaliation against employees who raise concerns in good faith or participate in a workplace review.
Employee 4 was advised to report any suspected retaliation promptly to Human Resources or another approved reporting channel.

Current Status

The complaint is currently under review. No final determination has been made.
The company will provide the employee with an appropriate status update when practical. The timing of the review may depend on witness availability, document collection, and the complexity of the concerns.

Recordkeeping

This complaint record and related documents will be maintained securely by Human Resources in accordance with company recordkeeping practices.

Next Review Date

Human Resources will review the status of this complaint by October 15, 2026.
