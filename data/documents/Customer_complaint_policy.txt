CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING PROCESS
Hypothetical Company: Northstar Services

Purpose

Northstar Services is committed to handling customer complaints fairly, promptly, and respectfully. Customer feedback helps us identify service issues, improve our processes, and strengthen customer relationships.

How Customers Can Submit a Complaint

Customers may submit complaints through:

- Email
- Telephone
- Online contact form
- In-person discussions with company staff
- Written correspondence

Customers should provide their name, contact information, a description of the issue, relevant dates, and any supporting documentation.

Complaint Acknowledgment

Northstar Services will acknowledge receipt of a complaint within two business days. The acknowledgment will include:

- The complaint reference number
- The name or team responsible for reviewing the complaint
- The expected timeline for the next update
- Any additional information needed from the customer

Complaint Review

The assigned representative will review the complaint objectively and gather relevant information. This may include:

- Reviewing customer records and communications
- Speaking with employees involved in the matter
- Checking applicable policies and procedures
- Requesting additional information from the customer
- Identifying whether similar complaints have occurred

The review will be conducted fairly and without retaliation against the customer.

Resolution and Response

Northstar Services aims to provide a final response within ten business days. If more time is required, the customer will receive an update explaining the reason for the delay and the revised expected completion date.

The final response will explain:

- What was reviewed
- The outcome of the review
- Any corrective action taken
- Any compensation or remedy offered, where appropriate
- How the customer may request an escalation or appeal

Escalation

If the customer is dissatisfied with the response, they may request an escalation to a manager or designated complaints officer. The escalation request should identify the reasons the customer believes the original response was incomplete or incorrect.

The escalation will be reviewed independently where practical, and Northstar Services will provide a further response within ten business days.

Corrective Action

When a complaint identifies a service failure, Northstar Services may take corrective action, such as:

- Correcting an error
- Replacing or repairing a product
- Providing a refund or credit, where appropriate
- Updating procedures
- Providing employee coaching or training
- Improving customer communications

Complaint Records and Confidentiality

Northstar Services will maintain records of complaints, investigations, decisions, and corrective actions. Complaint information will be handled confidentially and shared only with individuals who need it to review or resolve the matter.

Continuous Improvement

Complaint trends will be reviewed periodically by company management. The company will use this information to identify recurring issues, improve services, and reduce the likelihood of similar complaints in the future.

Customer Commitment

Northstar Services will treat every complaint seriously, listen respectfully, communicate clearly, and work toward a fair and timely resolution.
