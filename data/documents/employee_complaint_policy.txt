EMPLOYEE COMPLAINT POLICY

Company: Northstar Services
Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Policy Owner: Human Resources

1. Purpose

Northstar Services is committed to maintaining a respectful, fair, and professional workplace. This policy explains how employees can raise complaints, how complaints will be reviewed, and how the company will protect employees from retaliation.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, temporary workers, interns, managers, and supervisors working for or on behalf of Northstar Services.

3. Types of Complaints

Employees may submit complaints about matters including:

    - Workplace harassment, discrimination, or bullying
    - Retaliation or unfair treatment
    - Workplace safety concerns
    - Pay, scheduling, or leave issues
    - Conflicts with coworkers or managers
    - Violations of company policies
    - Unethical, dishonest, or inappropriate conduct
    - Concerns about working conditions
    - Misuse of company resources
    - Other workplace issues requiring management attention

Employees should raise concerns promptly when practical so the company can review and address them effectively.

4. How to Submit a Complaint

Employees may submit complaints through one of the following channels:

    - Directly to their manager
    - To Human Resources
    - To another member of management
    - Through the company s designated ethics or compliance reporting channel
    - Through any other reporting method communicated by the company

Employees are not required to report a complaint to a person involved in the concern. They may use another available reporting channel.

Complaints may be submitted verbally or in writing. Written complaints should include relevant facts, dates, locations, names of individuals involved, witnesses, and supporting documents when available.

5. Anonymous Complaints

Employees may submit complaints anonymously where the available reporting channel permits anonymous reporting. The company will review anonymous complaints to the extent possible.

Anonymous reporting may limit the company s ability to ask follow-up questions, verify information, or provide status updates.

6. Complaint Acknowledgment

Human Resources or the designated reviewer will acknowledge receipt of a complaint within two business days when the employee provides contact information.

The acknowledgment may include:

    - The complaint reference number
    - The person or team responsible for the review
    - The expected next steps
    - A request for additional information
    - The expected timing for an update

7. Review Process

The company will review complaints as promptly and fairly as practical. Depending on the nature of the complaint, the review may include:

    - Speaking with the reporting employee
    - Interviewing relevant witnesses
    - Reviewing documents, records, or communications
    - Consulting subject-matter or compliance personnel
    - Reviewing applicable policies and procedures
    - Providing the subject of the complaint an opportunity to respond

The company may adjust the review process based on the seriousness, complexity, and sensitivity of the matter.

8. Confidentiality

The company will handle complaints as confidentially as practical. Information may be shared with individuals who need it to review the complaint, respond to the concern, protect employees, or meet company obligations.

The company cannot guarantee complete confidentiality because an effective review may require speaking with witnesses or sharing relevant information.

Employees are expected to respect the confidentiality of complaint reviews and not interfere with the process.

9. Non-Retaliation

Northstar Services prohibits retaliation against any person who, in good faith:

    - Reports a concern
    - Participates in a review
    - Provides information or evidence
    - Requests assistance
    - Raises a question about company practices

Retaliation may include threats, intimidation, exclusion, schedule changes, loss of work opportunities, negative treatment, or other adverse conduct connected to the employee s report or participation.

Employees who believe they have experienced retaliation should report it promptly to Human Resources or another available reporting channel.

Knowingly making a false complaint or providing deliberately misleading information may result in corrective action. A complaint will not be considered improper merely because it cannot be substantiated.

10. Interim Measures

When appropriate, the company may take temporary steps while a complaint is being reviewed. These steps may include:

    - Adjusting reporting lines or work schedules
    - Separating employees involved in the concern
    - Providing workplace support
    - Restricting access to certain systems or locations
    - Taking steps to protect safety or preserve information

Interim measures are not conclusions about whether a complaint is substantiated.

11. Resolution and Outcome

At the conclusion of the review, the company will determine appropriate next steps based on the available information and applicable policies.

Possible actions may include:

    - Coaching or counseling
    - Additional training
    - Mediation or facilitated discussion
    - Process or workplace changes
    - Corrective action
    - Disciplinary action
    - Referral to another department or external authority, where appropriate

The company will communicate the conclusion to the reporting employee when practical. Due to privacy considerations, the company may not disclose specific employment actions involving another individual.

12. Timing

The company will aim to provide an initial update within ten business days of acknowledging a complaint. Complex complaints may require additional time.

If the review is delayed, the company will provide a status update when practical and explain the expected next step without disclosing confidential information.

13. Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

    - Report concerns honestly and in good faith
    - Provide accurate information
    - Preserve relevant records and evidence
    - Cooperate with reasonable review requests
    - Maintain confidentiality
    - Avoid retaliation or interference
    - Notify the company if circumstances change

14. Manager Responsibilities

Managers who receive an employee complaint must:

    - Listen respectfully and avoid making promises about the outcome
    - Document the concern accurately
    - Escalate the complaint to Human Resources or the appropriate reporting channel
    - Protect the employee from retaliation
    - Avoid conducting an informal investigation unless directed to do so
    - Preserve relevant information
    - Maintain confidentiality

Managers must not discourage employees from reporting concerns.

15. Recordkeeping

Human Resources or the designated policy owner will maintain appropriate records of complaints, reviews, decisions, and corrective actions.

Records will be stored securely and retained in accordance with the company s recordkeeping requirements.

16. Policy Review

Human Resources will review this policy periodically and update it as needed based on organizational changes, employee feedback, operational experience, and applicable requirements.

17. Employee Acknowledgment

Employees may be asked to acknowledge that they have received and reviewed this policy. Acknowledgment confirms receipt and understanding; it does not waive any employee rights or prevent an employee from raising a concern.

18. Contact Information

Employees should contact their manager, Human Resources, or the company s designated ethics and compliance reporting channel to raise a concern.
